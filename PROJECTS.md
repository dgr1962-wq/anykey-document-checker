## From working website to potential products

The clearest packaging candidates are the navigation and reading framework, conversation publishing tools, listening stations, and profile-guided build method. The live website demonstrates them in one environment. Turning them into products requires extraction, compatibility work, tests, documentation, licensing review, and a support plan. They are not being offered here as finished commercial packages.

For supporters, that distinction matters: there is tangible work to inspect, and a visible next stage to help complete.
